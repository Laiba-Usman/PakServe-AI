# Driving License Authority - International Driving Permit (IDP) Guide

## Overview
An **International Driving Permit (IDP)**, also commonly termed an International Driving License, is a recognized legal travel document issued under the United Nations Conventions on Road Traffic (1949 and 1968). It translates and certifies your Pakistani domestic driving license into multiple international languages (English, French, Spanish, Russian, Chinese, Arabic, etc.), allowing Pakistani citizens to legally rent and drive vehicles in over 150 convention signatory countries worldwide.

International Driving Permits are issued by City / Provincial Traffic Police Licensing Authorities (such as Punjab Traffic Police / DLIMS, Sindh Traffic Police, Islamabad Traffic Police - ITP, and Khyber Pakhtunkhwa Traffic Police).

---

## Eligibility Criteria
- Applicant must hold an **active, valid Permanent Driving License** (Car / Motorcycle) issued by any Pakistani provincial traffic licensing authority.
- The applicant's domestic driving license must not be expired, suspended, or revoked.
- Applicant must hold a valid Pakistani Passport with a valid foreign visa / entry stamp or confirmed return air ticket.
- Applicant must be at least 18 years of age.
- Holders of only a Learner Driving Permit are **NOT** eligible for an International Driving Permit.

---

## Required Documents Checklist
1. **Original Valid Pakistani Permanent Driving License** (plus 2 clear photocopies).
2. **Original Valid CNIC / Smart NIC / NICOP** (plus 2 photocopies).
3. **Original Valid Pakistani Passport** (valid for at least 6 months) + 2 photocopies of bio-data page and valid foreign visa.
4. **Copy of Valid Foreign Visa / Residence Permit / Air Ticket**.
5. **Passport-Sized Photographs**: 2 recent photos (size: 2x2 inches / standard passport size, white or blue background).
6. **Paid Fee Challan / PSID Receipt**: Paid via online banking / e-Pay or bank counter.
7. **Medical Fitness Certificate (Form-B)**: Required if applicant is aged 50+ or if specified by regional traffic rules.

---

## Step-by-Step Issuance Procedure

### In-Person Application at Traffic Police Licensing Center / Police Khidmat Markaz:
1. **Visit Center**: Go to an authorized International Driving License branch or Police Khidmat Markaz (PKM) in your division.
2. **Token Generation**: Collect an "International Driving Permit" service token at the reception desk.
3. **Document Submission**: Present original Passport, National CNIC, and Permanent Domestic Driving License to the data verification officer.
4. **Biometric & Photograph**: Undergo live facial photo capture and digital thumbprint scan.
5. **Fee Payment**: Pay the prescribed IDP fee at the on-site cash counter or via 1Link PSID barcode.
6. **Card & Booklet Issuance**: The International Driving Permit is prepared in the official multi-page UN booklet format.
7. **Collection**: In most major cities (Islamabad, Lahore, Karachi, Rawalpindi), the IDP booklet is handed over on the **same day within 1 to 2 hours**, or delivered via courier within 2 to 4 business days.

|---|---|---|---|
| International Driving Permit (Booklet) | Exactly 1 Year from Issue Date | Rs. 2,000 - Rs. 4,500 | Rs. 5,000 - Rs. 8,000 |

*Note: Under international UN conventions, an IDP is valid for a maximum duration of **1 year** from the date of issuance or until the expiration date of your domestic driving license, whichever occurs earlier.*

---

## Important Rules for International Driving
- **Always Carry Both Documents**: When driving abroad, you must carry **BOTH** your International Driving Permit booklet AND your original Pakistani domestic plastic driving license card. An IDP is not a standalone license; it is a translation that validates your primary domestic license.
- **Permanent Residents / Students**: In many countries (e.g. UK, USA, EU states, Australia, UAE), an IDP is legally valid for tourists and short-term business visitors for up to 90 to 365 days. If you become a permanent resident or register as a long-term student, you are generally required to convert to a local driver's license within a mandated timeframe (usually 3 to 12 months).
- Disclaimer: Regulations and fees are illustrative for demo guidance. Verify specific country requirements and local traffic police portals (e.g. dlims.punjab.gov.pk / itp.gov.pk).
