# Driving License Authority (DLIMS / Traffic Police) - Learner Driving Permit Guide

## Overview
A Learner Driving Permit is the mandatory first step for any individual seeking to operate a motor vehicle on public roads in Pakistan. The permit authorizes the holder to practice driving under the supervision of a licensed driver holding a valid permanent license for that vehicle category.

Provincial Traffic Police departments (e.g. Punjab DLIMS, Sindh Driving License Branch, KPK Traffic Police, Balochistan, and Islamabad Traffic Police - ITP) manage issuance. Across most provinces, learner permits can now be acquired both **online** through official web portals (such as dlims.punjab.gov.pk) and **in-person** at Police Khidmat Marakaz (PKM) / Traffic Licensing Centers.

---

## Eligibility Criteria & Age Limits
- **Motorcycle / Motor Car / Jeep (Non-Commercial)**: Minimum 18 years of age.
- **Auto Rickshaw / Light Transport Vehicle (LTV / Taxi)**: Minimum 21 years of age.
- **Heavy Transport Vehicle (HTV / Public Service Vehicle - PSV)**: Minimum 22 years of age (requires existing valid LTV license held for at least 3 years).
- Applicant must be a Pakistani citizen with a valid CNIC / Smart ID card or a foreigner with valid passport/visa.

---

## Required Documents Checklist
1. **Original Valid CNIC / Smart NIC** (along with 1 clear photocopy).
2. **Medical Fitness Certificate (Form-B)**: Required for applicants aged 50+ or for commercial vehicle categories (LTV/HTV).
3. **Passport Size Photograph**: 1 recent photograph with blue/white background (captured live digitally at most licensing centers).
4. **Paid Fee Challan / PSID**: Generated online or paid via e-Pay Punjab / Sindh e-Payment / ITP counters.
5. **Blood Group Record**: Stated or verified via laboratory report.

---

## Application Channels & Step-by-Step Procedure

### 1. Online Issuance (e.g., Punjab DLIMS Portal - dlims.punjab.gov.pk)
1. Visit the official licensing portal and register with your CNIC, mobile number, and email.
2. Select "Apply for Learner Permit".
3. Enter CNIC number, date of birth, blood group, current address, and select vehicle category (Motorcycle, Car/Jeep, LTV, etc.).
4. Upload a clear photograph (under 500KB) and scanned copy of front and back of CNIC.
5. Generate PSID code for online fee payment.
6. Pay fee using 1Link / Easypaisa / JazzCash / ATM / Mobile Banking App.
7. Once payment status reflects "Paid", download and print the official Computerized Learner Driving Permit instantly with embedded QR code.

### 2. In-Person at Police Khidmat Markaz (PKM) / Traffic Headquarters
1. Walk in to the nearest Police Khidmat Markaz, Traffic Police Driving License Center, or mobile licensing van.
2. Obtain a token from the automated ticketing queue.
3. Present original CNIC at the data capture desk.
4. Live photo and eye-sight / color-blindness test is conducted on-site.
5. Pay ticket fee at the counter.
6. Learner permit is printed on secure laminated card/paper and handed over immediately.

## Fee Structure and Validity

| Vehicle Category | Permit Fee (Annual) | Validity Period | Mandatory Practice Period |
|---|---|---|---|
| Motorcycle Only | Rs. 500 - Rs. 1,000 | 6 Months / 1 Year | Minimum 42 Days |
| Motor Car / Jeep | Rs. 500 - Rs. 1,000 | 6 Months / 1 Year | Minimum 42 Days |
| Motorcycle + Car/Jeep (Combined) | Rs. 1,000 - Rs. 1,500 | 6 Months / 1 Year | Minimum 42 Days |
| LTV / Commercial | Rs. 1,000 - Rs. 2,000 | 6 Months | Minimum 42 Days |

*The learner permit is valid for 6 months across most provinces. A mandatory waiting period of **42 days** from the date of permit issuance is legally required before an applicant can appear for the permanent driving license test.*

---

## Driving Regulations for Learner Drivers
- A prominent red **"L"** sign (measuring at least 4 inches high) must be affixed to the front and rear windshield / body of the practicing vehicle.
- A learner driver must always be accompanied in the passenger seat by a licensed driver with an active permanent driving license for that category.
- Carrying pillion passengers on a motorcycle while on a learner permit is strictly prohibited (except for a licensed instructor).
- Disclaimer: Regulations and fees are illustrative for demo guidance. Consult local provincial Traffic Police / DLIMS portals for exact regional fee schedules.
