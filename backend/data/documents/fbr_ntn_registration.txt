# Federal Board of Revenue (FBR) - National Tax Number (NTN) Registration Guide

## Overview
The National Tax Number (NTN) is a unique tax identifier issued by the Federal Board of Revenue (FBR), Government of Pakistan. Obtaining an NTN is the primary legal step toward becoming a registered taxpayer, opening corporate bank accounts, conducting high-value property or vehicle purchases at reduced tax withholding rates, and obtaining an Active Taxpayer List (ATL) "Filer" status.

Under the modernized FBR IRIS e-Services system, an individual's **13-digit CNIC number serves directly as their NTN**. Online registration takes less than 10 minutes and is completely **free of charge**.

---

## Types of NTN Registration
1. **Individual Salaried NTN**: For individuals earning income primarily from employment / salary.
2. **Individual Business (Sole Proprietorship) NTN**: For self-employed professionals, freelancers, sole owners of small and medium businesses.
3. **Association of Persons (AOP / Partnership) NTN**: For registered partnership firms with multiple partners.
4. **Corporate Company (Pvt Ltd / Public Ltd) NTN**: For corporate entities registered under the Securities and Exchange Commission of Pakistan (SECP).

---

## Required Information and Documents

### For Individual (Salaried / Unregistered Sole Owner):
- **13-digit CNIC / SNIC Number**.
- **Personal Active Mobile Number**: Must be registered in the applicant's own name/CNIC with a telecom operator (required for SMS OTP verification).
- **Personal Active Email Address**: For receiving system activation codes and official tax notices.
- **Residential Address Details** and recent paid utility bill (Electricity or Gas) consumer number for address proof.

### For Sole Proprietorship / Business Individual:
- **Business Name** and Date of Commencement.
- **Principal Business Activity** (selected from standard industrial classification codes).
- **Business Premises Ownership Proof** (Paid utility bill in business name / landlord name, or registered Rent/Lease Agreement).
- **Letterhead and Business Rubber Stamp**.

### For AOPs and Private Limited Companies:
- Partnership Deed and Form-C (for AOPs) OR Certificate of Incorporation and Memorandum & Articles of Association (for SECP Companies).
- CNICs and NTNs of all Partners / Directors.
- Board Resolution appointing Principal Officer / Authorized Representative.

## Step-by-Step Online Registration via FBR IRIS Portal

1. **Access Portal**: Visit the official FBR IRIS 2.0 portal at `iris.fbr.gov.pk`.
2. **Select Registration**: Click on the **"Registration for Unregistered Person"** link on the login screen.
3. **Enter Basic Bio-Data**:
   - Provide CNIC number, nationality, name prefix, first name, and surname.
   - Enter your personal Cell Phone Number and Email Address.
4. **OTP Verification**:
   - The IRIS system sends two distinct verification codes: one via SMS to your mobile phone, and one via Email.
   - Enter both OTP codes into the verification boxes and submit.
5. **Receive Login Credentials**:
   - Once verified, FBR instantly transmits your **Registration Number (your CNIC)**, default **Password**, and a 4-digit confidential **PIN** to your registered SMS and Email.
6. **Log in to IRIS**:
   - Log in using your CNIC and received password.
   - To add a business name or bank account: Navigate to **Form 181 (Application for modification in Income Tax Registration)**.
   - Add business particulars, commercial address, and IBAN (International Bank Account Number).
   - Submit Form 181 with your 4-digit electronic PIN.
7. **Download NTN / Registration Certificate**:
   - Navigate to the "Print" / "Registration Certificate" option on IRIS dashboard.
   - Download and save your official 181 Order / Taxpayer Registration Certificate PDF.

## Fee and Processing Time
- **Official FBR Registration Fee**: **Rs. 0 (100% Free)**.
- **Processing Time**: Instantaneous (System generates credentials automatically within minutes upon OTP confirmation).

---

## Key Benefits of Having an NTN and Active Taxpayer Status
- **Lower Withholding Tax**: Active taxpayers (Filers) enjoy 50% to 100% lower withholding tax rates on banking transactions, dividend income, prize bonds, vehicle registration, and real estate property sales/purchases compared to non-filers.
- **Formal Credit Access**: Mandatory requirement for obtaining commercial loans, car financing, and business credit lines.
- **Legal Compliance**: Avoids bank account freezing, non-filer penal notices, and mobile SIM blockages introduced under national tax compliance frameworks.
