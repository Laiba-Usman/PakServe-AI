# NADRA - Family Registration Certificate (FRC) Issuance Guide

## Overview
A **Family Registration Certificate (FRC)** is an official document issued by NADRA that validates the applicant's family composition and structural tree registered in NADRA's centralized database. 

FRC is widely demanded for:
- Foreign Visa applications (Schengen, UK, US, Canada, Australia, Gulf countries)
- Immigration and Family Reunification cases
- Legal inheritance, succession, and property division settlements
- Court matters requiring verified legal heirship

---

## Types of Family Registration Certificates (FRC)
NADRA provides three distinct categories of FRC based on applicant requirement:

1. **FRC by Birth (Lineage with Parents & Siblings)**:
   - Lists the applicant along with their Father, Mother, Brothers, and Sisters.
   - Used predominantly by unmarried individuals or for inheritance claims tracing to parental estate.
2. **FRC by Marriage (Immediate Nuclear Family)**:
   - Lists the applicant along with their Spouse (Husband/Wife) and Children.
   - Used by married couples for spouse visas, family visit visas, or children sponsorship.
3. **FRC by Adoption**:
   - Lists adopted children with legal guardian/adoptive parents pursuant to a valid court adoption decree.

---

## Prerequisites and Eligibility
- All family members listed in the requested certificate must be registered in the NADRA database with valid CNICs (for adults) or B-Forms/CRC (for minors under 18).
- If any family member's data is incomplete or has unlinked parentage in the database, the FRC cannot be printed until that record is corrected.
- Applicant must be a registered member within that specific family tree.

---

## Application Channels & Procedure

### 1. In-Person at NADRA Registration Center (NRC) / Executive Center:
- Visit any NRC or Mega Center with your original CNIC.
- Take an "FRC" service token.
- Provide the CNIC numbers of your parents/siblings (for By Birth) or spouse/children (for By Marriage).
- The operator retrieves the family tree on screen for visual verification by the applicant.
- Applicant provides live thumbprint biometric verification.
- Pay the certificate fee at the counter.
- The FRC is printed instantly on high-security watermark paper with an official QR code and handed to the applicant within 15-30 minutes.

### 2. Online via Pak-ID Portal (id.nadra.gov.pk):
- Log in to your Pak-ID account.
- Select "Family Registration Certificate (FRC)".
- Choose category (By Birth or By Marriage) and enter applicant CNIC.
- System automatically retrieves all linked family records.
- If photographs of minor children are missing, upload passport-sized digital photos.
- Provide applicant biometric verification (via Pak-ID mobile app).
- Pay fee via credit/debit card.
- A secure PDF version of the FRC with digital signature and verification QR code is emailed directly to the applicant within 24 to 48 hours.

## Key Notes and Troubleshooting
- **Missing Family Member**: If a sibling or child does not show up in the family tree, they must first update their B-Form or CNIC linking their parents' CNIC numbers before the FRC will reflect them.
- **Deceased Relatives**: Deceased parents or spouses will show status as 'Deceased' provided their death certificates were properly registered with NADRA.
- **Embassy Attestation**: For certain embassies, the FRC printed by NADRA does not require additional Ministry of Foreign Affairs (MOFA) attestation due to the embedded verification QR code, though checking embassy-specific visa rules is recommended.
