# District Administration - Domicile Certificate Issuance & Verification Guide

## Overview
A **Domicile Certificate** is a statutory legal document issued under the Pakistan Citizenship Act, 1951 (and Domicile Rules, 1952) by the District Administration (Deputy Commissioner / District Magistrate / Assistant Commissioner) certifying that a citizen is a permanent resident of a specific district and province in Pakistan.

The Domicile Certificate is mandatory for:
- Claiming provincial and regional quota reservations in Federal and Provincial Civil Services (FPSC / PPSC / SPSC / KPPSC / BPSC exams).
- Admission to government medical colleges, engineering universities, and professional public institutions under district merit quotas.
- Applying for provincial government employment and statutory welfare schemes.

---

## Eligibility Criteria
- Applicant must be a bona fide resident of the district for a specified minimum period (generally at least 1 to 3 continuous years).
- Applicant must possess a valid CNIC (for adults 18+) or B-Form (for minors under 18) with a permanent address matching the respective district.
- An individual can legally hold **ONLY ONE** Domicile Certificate in Pakistan. Holding dual domiciles from two different districts is a criminal offense under the Pakistan Penal Code.

---

## Required Documents Checklist
1. **Application Form (Form-A / Form-P-1)**: Duly filled with applicant particulars.
2. **Original CNIC / Smart NIC / B-Form** + 3 photocopies.
3. **Father's / Mother's / Guardian's CNIC** + photocopies.
4. **Father's Domicile Certificate** (if available; highly recommended for minor or student applicants).
5. **Educational Certificates**:
   - Matriculation / Intermediate / Primary school certificates establishing continuous study in the district.
6. **Proof of Residence / Property Ownership in the District**:
   - Land ownership record (Fard / Registry / Inteqal) in applicant or parents' name, OR registered residential rent deed, OR utility bill (Electricity/Sui Gas) of the district.
7. **Passport-Sized Photographs**: 3 to 4 clear recent photographs with light background.
8. **Affidavit on Non-Judicial Stamp Paper (Rs. 50 - 100)**: Attested by an Oath Commissioner, declaring under oath that the applicant is a permanent resident of the district and does not possess a domicile from any other district or province.
9. **Paid Challan (Head C-02684 / Judicial Fee)**: Paid at National Bank of Pakistan (NBP) or District Treasury.

## Step-by-Step Issuance Procedure

### Option 1: Digital / E-Khidmat Markaz (e.g. Punjab e-Khidmat / Domicile Management System - DMS)
1. **Visit Center / Portal**: Visit the District Citizen Facilitation Center (e-Khidmat Markaz / Khidmat Center) or district DMS web portal.
2. **Token Generation**: Collect a "Domicile" service token.
3. **Data & Biometric Capture**:
   - Operator scans educational proofs, CNIC, and utility bills into the digital system.
   - Digital photograph and thumbprints are acquired.
4. **Tehsildar / Halqa Patwari Verification**:
   - The revenue authority (Halqa Patwari / Kanungo / Tehsildar) verifies land/residence claims electronically or through field reporting.
5. **Approval by Assistant Commissioner / Deputy Commissioner**:
   - The authorized District Magistrate or AC approves the electronic certificate.
6. **Certificate Printing & Handover**:
   - Domicile Certificate is printed on secure security watermark paper with an embedded QR code for electronic verification.
   - Ready within 3 to 7 working days.

### Option 2: Traditional District Court / DC Office Track
1. Obtain Form-A from the District Kutchery / DC Office revenue section.
2. Get the form attested by Halqa Patwari and Tehsildar with official revenue stamp.
3. Submit application along with paid NBP treasury challan at the DC Office Domicile Branch.
4. Appear for brief verification interview before the Assistant Commissioner or designated Magistrate.
5. Collect the stamped Domicile Certificate upon receipt.

## Key Rules & Cancellation
- **Transfer of Domicile**: If a citizen permanently moves their domicile to another district, they must first apply for formal cancellation and surrender of their previous district domicile before a new certificate can be legally granted.
- **Online Verification**: Most modern domiciles feature a QR code that can be scanned by universities and public service commissions to confirm authenticity instantly.
- Disclaimer: Procedures and fee figures are illustrative for demo guidance. Verify with the local District Deputy Commissioner Office.
