# Excise, Taxation & Narcotics Control Department - Standard Computerized Number Plates & Replacement Guide

## Overview
Provincial Motor Registration Authorities in Pakistan issue standardized, high-security computerized number plates embossed with official provincial emblems, retro-reflective sheeting, unique serial numbers, and laser-etched security marks to ensure vehicle identification and prevent vehicle duplication or auto theft.

Driving with non-standard, fancy, tinted, folded, or unreadable aftermarket number plates is a cognizable offense under the Motor Vehicles Ordinance, attracting traffic challans, impoundment of number plates, and heavy fines.

---

## When to Apply for Number Plates?
1. **Fresh Registration**: Number plates are automatically ordered as part of the initial vehicle registration package.
2. **Replacement of Lost Plates**: If one or both number plates are stolen or lost on the road.
3. **Replacement of Damaged / Faded Plates**: If plates have suffered physical collision damage or weathering.

---

## Required Documents Checklist

### For Replacement of Lost Number Plates:
1. **Police Report / Daily Diary (Roznamcha Entry / FIR)**: Obtained from the local Police Station reporting the loss or theft of the specific number plate.
2. **Original Vehicle Registration Smart Card / Book** + 1 photocopy.
3. **Original CNIC of Registered Vehicle Owner** + photocopy.
4. **Affidavit on Rs. 50 - 100 Stamp Paper**: Attested by an Oath Commissioner, declaring that the plate was genuinely lost and will not be misused.
5. **Paid Fee Challan / PSID**: Generated online or at the Excise bank counter.

### For Replacement of Damaged / Broken Plates:
1. **Surrender of Damaged Number Plates**: Both old plates must be physically handed over to the Excise Motor Branch for official cancellation and destruction.
2. **Original Smart Card / Registration Book**.
3. **Original CNIC of Owner**.
4. **Paid Fee Receipt**.

---

## Application Procedure for Replacement Plates

1. **Lodge Police Report (For Lost Plates)**:
   - Report loss at the local police station to obtain the signed Roznamcha copy.
2. **Generate PSID / Challan**:
   - Access provincial Excise portal / e-Pay app or visit the Motor Registration Authority (MRA) counter.
   - Select "Duplicate / Replacement Number Plate".
   - Pay the prescribed fee via 1Link / mobile banking.
3. **Submit Application at Excise Motor Branch**:
   - Present the Roznamcha report / damaged plates, affidavit, CNIC copy, and paid challan.
   - The Excise Officer verifies the vehicle file and places a production order with the government manufacturing facility (e.g. National Radio and Telecommunication Corporation - NRTC / Provincial Printing Facility).
4. **Temporary Driving Permit**:
   - The applicant is issued a temporary stamped authorization receipt permitting vehicle operation while new plates are fabricated.
5. **Collection of New Plates**:
   - Upon receiving SMS notification (typically within 15 to 30 working days), collect the new embossed number plates from the Excise office upon presenting the original receipt.

## Regulations Regarding Universal & Personalized Number Plates
- **Universal Numbering System**: Most provinces (such as Punjab and Sindh) issue universal alphanumeric formats (e.g. `LEA-24-1234` or `AAA-123`) valid province-wide without district segregation.
- **Auction of Attractive / Vanity Numbers**: Special attractive numbers (e.g. `1`, `786`, `007`, `999`) or customized vanity plates are auctioned periodically via online e-Auction portals with reserve bidding prices.
