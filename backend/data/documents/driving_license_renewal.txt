# Driving License Authority (DLIMS / Traffic Police) - Driving License Renewal Guide

## Overview
A Permanent Driving License in Pakistan is issued with a fixed validity of either 3, 5, or 10 years. Drivers are legally obligated to renew their license upon expiry. Driving with an expired license constitutes a moving traffic violation under the Motor Vehicles Ordinance, attracting traffic challans, vehicle impoundment, and potential invalidation of motor insurance claims during accidents.

Renewal can be carried out both **online** (via provincial DLIMS portals such as dlims.punjab.gov.pk or Sindh / Islamabad apps) and **in-person** at Police Khidmat Marakaz (PKM) or Traffic Police Licensing Centers.

---

## Grace Period and Late Renewal Penalties
Traffic rules provide specific renewal windows and late penalty tiers:
- **Within 30 Days of Expiry**: No penalty. Normal renewal fee applies.
- **Between 1 Month and 1 Year of Expiry**: Standard renewal fee + small late surcharge (approx 25% to 50% extra).
- **Between 1 Year and 3 Years of Expiry**: Standard renewal fee + heavy penalty (approx 100% fine).
- **Expired for More Than 3 Years**: The license is cancelled from active database status. The applicant is treated as a fresh candidate and must obtain a new Learner Permit, wait 42 days, and re-take both the theoretical sign test and practical driving track test.

---

## Required Documents Checklist
1. **Original Expired Driving License** (or legible front/back copy).
2. **Original Valid CNIC / Smart NIC** + 1 photocopy.
3. **Medical Fitness Certificate (Form-B)**:
   - Mandatory for commercial categories (LTV/HTV/PSV).
   - Mandatory for all non-commercial drivers aged 50 years and above.
4. **Passport Size Photograph**: 1 photo (for physical center submission; captured digitally or uploaded for online apps).
5. **Paid Renewal Fee Receipt / PSID**: Generated online or paid via designated banking channels.

---

## Renewal Procedures

### 1. Online License Renewal (e.g. DLIMS Portal):
1. Log in to the provincial DLIMS web portal or mobile app.
2. Select "Renew Driving License".
3. Enter CNIC number and existing License number.
4. Confirm personal details, updated mobile number, and delivery mailing address.
5. Upload a clear frontal photo and scanned copy of CNIC (front and back) and old license.
6. Generate PSID code and pay the renewal fee + courier charges via 1Link / ATM / Easypaisa / JazzCash / Mobile Banking.
7. Download your digital renewal confirmation receipt and e-License with QR code.
8. The physical Smart Driving License card is printed and delivered by postal courier within 7 to 15 business days.

### 2. In-Person Renewal at Police Khidmat Markaz (PKM):
1. Visit the nearest PKM or Traffic Police center.
2. Collect a renewal token at the reception desk.
3. Submit old license and CNIC at the renewal window.
4. Complete biometric authentication and live photo capture.
5. Pay the fee challan at the integrated bank counter / digital terminal.
6. Retain token receipt. The renewed physical card will be delivered to your residence.

## Important Rules & Notes
- An applicant does not need to re-take any driving or sign tests for regular license renewals (provided the license has not been expired for more than 3 years).
- A renewed e-License stored on your mobile phone via the official Traffic Police app is legally valid for road inspection across all national highways and city roads.
- Disclaimer: Procedures and fee figures are illustrative for demo guidance. Verify with official provincial Traffic Police authorities.
