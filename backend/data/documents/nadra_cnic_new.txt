# NADRA - New Computerized National Identity Card (CNIC / SNIC) Issuance Guide

## Overview and Eligibility
The Computerized National Identity Card (CNIC) or Smart National Identity Card (SNIC) is the primary proof of citizenship and identity for Pakistani nationals aged 18 and above. A first-time applicant (fresh card) must visit a National Database and Registration Authority (NADRA) Registration Center (NRC) in Pakistan or apply via the Pak-ID online portal if residing abroad (NICOP).

### Eligibility Criteria:
- Applicant must be a citizen of Pakistan.
- Applicant must have attained the age of 18 years.
- Applicant must possess a valid Child Registration Certificate (CRC / B-Form) or standard Birth Certificate issued by the Union Council.
- At least one blood relative (father, mother, brother, sister, son, or daughter) with a valid CNIC must accompany the applicant for physical biometric attestation, OR a valid attested form signed by a Gazetted Officer (Grade 16 or above / elected representative / Nazim) is required.

---

## Required Documents
1. **Child Registration Certificate (CRC / B-Form)** or Union Council Birth Certificate (Original + 1 copy).
2. **CNIC of Parents**: Original CNIC or copy of Father's and Mother's valid CNIC.
3. **Presence of Blood Relative**: Physical presence of father, mother, brother, or sister holding an active CNIC for instant biometric verification at the counter.
4. **Alternative Attestation**: In the absence of a blood relative, an application form printed at the NRC counter must be attested by a Gazetted Officer (Grade 16+) / Councilor / Nazim / Member of Parliament.
5. **Educational / Matriculation Certificate** (Optional, recommended for accurate date of birth verification if discrepancies arise).
6. **Marriage Certificate (Nikkah Nama)** if the applicant is married and applying as head or under husband's name.

---

## Step-by-Step Application Procedure at NADRA Registration Center (NRC)

1. **Token Issuance**:
   - Visit the nearest NADRA Registration Center (NRC), Mega Center, or Executive Center.
   - Enter your CNIC/B-Form number at the reception kiosk to receive a token number.
2. **Biometrics & Data Entry**:
   - When your token is called, proceed to the data entry counter.
   - The data entry operator will capture your live photograph, digital fingerprints (all 10 fingers), and digital signature.
   - All personal details (Full Name, Father's Name, Date of Birth, Gender, Permanent and Present Addresses, Marital Status) are entered.
3. **Biometric Verification / Attestation**:
   - The accompanying blood relative will place their thumb on the biometric scanner to verify family lineage and approve the application instantly.
4. **Review and Confirmation**:
   - A draft form is printed (Bay-Form/Application summary). Carefully review all spellings, addresses, and date of birth.
   - Sign the verified summary.
5. **Fee Payment**:
   - Pay the applicable fee at the counter (Cash, Debit/Credit Card, or e-Sahulat).
   - Collect the tracking receipt containing the 12-digit tracking ID.
6. **Card Collection / Delivery**:
   - Track application status via SMS by sending your tracking ID to 8400 or checking the Pak-ID portal.
   - Collect the card in person from the center upon presentation of the token receipt, or receive it via courier (TCS/Pakistan Post) if home delivery was selected.

## Fee Structure and Processing Timelines

| Category / Type | Normal Track | Urgent Track | Executive Track |
|---|---|---|---|
| Plain CNIC (Chip-less) | Free (First Time) / Rs. 750 | Rs. 1,150 | Rs. 2,150 |
| Smart National ID Card (SNIC with Chip) | Rs. 750 | Rs. 1,500 | Rs. 2,500 |
| Estimated Delivery Time | 30 Working Days | 15 Working Days | 7 Working Days |

*Note: Executive and Mega Centers charge additional counter convenience fees. Fees are subject to revision by the Ministry of Interior/NADRA.*

---

## Important Guidelines
- Verify both the **Present Address** and **Permanent Address** carefully. Permanent address must match district domicile records or family revenue records.
- For citizens with physical disabilities (Special Persons), a special logo (wheelchair symbol) is printed on the CNIC upon presenting a disability certificate from the Medical Board / Social Welfare Department.
- For transgender individuals, NADRA offers Guru registration or biological parent linkage options under special facilitation desks.
- Disclaimer: Procedural requirements and fee amounts are illustrative for public guidance. Verify with official NADRA channels (helpline 1777 or 051-111-786-100) before visiting.
