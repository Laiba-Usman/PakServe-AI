# Driving License Authority (DLIMS / Traffic Police) - Regular / Permanent Driving License Issuance

## Overview
A Permanent Computerized Driving License authorizes a citizen to independently operate specified categories of motor vehicles (Motorcycle, Car/Jeep, LTV, HTV, Tractor, etc.) on public thoroughfares across Pakistan. 

To convert a Learner Driving Permit into a full Permanent Driving License, the applicant must complete the mandatory 42-day practice period, clear a Computerized Sign Test (Theory / Road Signs), and pass an On-Field Practical Driving Test (Physical driving track maneuver test) conducted by Traffic Police testing officers.

---

## Eligibility Criteria
- Completed mandatory minimum **42 days** from the issuance date of the active Learner Permit.
- Minimum age:
  - 18 years for Motorcycle and Non-Commercial Motor Car / Jeep.
  - 21 years for Light Transport Vehicle (LTV) / Rickshaw.
  - 22 years for Heavy Transport Vehicle (HTV) (requires 3 years prior LTV driving experience).
- Medically fit with adequate visual acuity (tested through medical examination or on-site eyesight screening).

---

## Required Documents Checklist
1. **Original Active Learner Driving Permit** (must be at least 42 days old).
2. **Original Valid CNIC / SNIC** + 2 photocopies.
3. **Medical Fitness Certificate (Form-B)**: Signed and stamped by a registered medical practitioner (mandatory for commercial categories LTV/HTV and candidates aged 50+).
4. **Passport-Sized Photographs**: 2 recent passport-size photos with blue/white background (if not captured digitally at center).
5. **Paid Application Ticket / Challan Receipt**: Form fee + licensing fee paid via automated PSID or licensing bank counter.
6. **Vehicle for Practical Test**: Candidate must bring their own road-worthy vehicle corresponding to the applied category (or pay a nominal fee to use traffic school test vehicles where available).

---

## Step-by-Step Testing & Licensing Process

### Stage 1: Theory Test (Sign & Road Rules Test)
1. Report to the Traffic Police Testing Center (e.g. PKM / DLIMS testing track / ITP Center).
2. Present your CNIC and 42-day completed Learner Permit at the registration counter.
3. Sit at a Computerized Touchscreen Testing Kiosk.
4. The test presents a series of multiple-choice questions on traffic signs (Mandatory, Warning, Informatory) and right-of-way rules.
5. Minimum passing score is typically 80% (e.g. answering at least 8 out of 10 questions correctly within 3 minutes).
6. Result is displayed instantly on screen. If passed, proceed to Stage 2.

### Stage 2: Practical Field Driving Test
1. Candidate reports to the driving track with test vehicle.
2. The practical test evaluates:
   - **Pre-drive inspection**: Mirror adjustment, seatbelt fastening, handbrake release, and turn indicator usage.
   - **Parallel / Perpendicular Parking**: Reversing into a defined cone-marked parking bay without touching boundary cones.
   - **"L-Shape" / "S-Shape" Reverse Track**: Driving forward through a designated track and reversing smoothly all the way back without halting the engine, exceeding boundary lines, or touching cones.
   - **Slope / Gradient Test** (in certain centers): Starting smoothly on an incline without rolling backward.
3. The Traffic Inspector marks the candidate as "Pass" or "Fail".

### Stage 3: Card Production and Delivery
1. Upon clearing both tests, the officer stamps the passing dossier.
2. Candidate proceeds to the delivery desk, verifies delivery address, and completes biometric authorization.
3. The Smart Chip Driving License is dispatched to the applicant's residential address via Pakistan Post (EMS / UMS) or courier within 7 to 14 working days.
4. A digital e-License can also be downloaded immediately from the DLIMS portal/app.

## Fee Structure and Validity

| Category | 5-Year License Fee | 10-Year License Fee |
|---|---|---|
| Motorcycle | Rs. 1,000 - Rs. 1,800 | Rs. 2,000 - Rs. 3,000 |
| Motor Car / Jeep | Rs. 1,800 - Rs. 3,000 | Rs. 3,500 - Rs. 5,000 |
| Motorcycle + Car (Combined) | Rs. 2,500 - Rs. 4,000 | Rs. 4,500 - Rs. 7,000 |
| LTV / HTV | Rs. 3,000 - Rs. 5,000 | N/A (Subject to medical re-certification) |

*Card delivery courier fee (approx Rs. 200 - Rs. 300) is charged separately at checkout.*

---

## What Happens if You Fail the Test?
- If an applicant fails the Theory Sign Test, they can re-appear after **48 hours to 7 days** depending on provincial rules.
- If an applicant fails the Practical Track Test, they must wait a mandatory re-test period of **15 to 42 days** before attempting again.
- No new learner permit is required during re-tests as long as the original permit remains valid.
- Disclaimer: Procedures and fee schedules are illustrative for demo guidance. Verify with your provincial DLIMS / Traffic Police.
